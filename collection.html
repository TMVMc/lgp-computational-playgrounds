LET’S GET PHYGITAL

Computational Playgrounds

A series of interactive studies of repetition, variation, and form. Move the controls to see each rule at work. Each study includes notes for building a related setup in Grasshopper or Blender.

01

Fields of Influence

An attractor field: distance from a movable point controls the size of circles across a grid.

02

Waves

Change the height, repetition, and offset of a set of waves.

03

Phyllotaxis

A small change in the turning angle can rearrange the entire spiral.

04

Noise

Disturb a regular grid with a field of smoothly changing values.

05

Cellular Systems

Each row follows from the one above it. Change the rule or the starting cells to see a different pattern.

06

Vector Fields

Combine attraction, rotation, and drift to change the direction at every point.

07

Growth

Start with one tip. At each step it extends, turns, and may split into two.

08

Morphing

Move between a circle and a star by blending their point positions.

09

Curve Distribution

Place repeated elements along an ellipse and turn them to follow the curve.

10

Tiling

Repeat squares or hexagons, open gaps, and resize alternate cells.

11

Surface Deformation

Bend, twist, and taper a strip. Switch the order to see how the operations interact.

12

Scatter

Fill a boundary with shapes and vary their scale and rotation.

13

3D Scatter

Place forms on a surface or inside a volume, then orbit the view.

The playgrounds run in your browser. The modeling notes assume some familiarity with Grasshopper or Blender; they outline the setup rather than provide tested project files.
